# HAY2010 website — wayfinder map

> Canonical tracker: GitHub issue [#1](https://github.com/mustaphaelou/hay2010website/issues/1). This file is a local mirror; the local-to-GitHub mapping is in [`github-issues.md`](github-issues.md).

## Destination

A build-ready specification for the HAY2010 French corporate website, adapted from `lamalifgroup.ma`'s information architecture and section patterns, plus the decisions needed to start implementation in the existing Next.js 16 repo. The resolved tickets are the spec; this map does not write production code.

## Notes

- **Domain:** corporate website for HAY2010 SARL, a Rabat electrical contractor working in public lighting, HTA/BT electrification, transformer stations, CFO/CFA and solar.
- **Reference architecture:** `lamalifgroup.ma`. Teardown: [`docs/research/lamalifgroup-architecture-style.md`](../../docs/research/lamalifgroup-architecture-style.md). Company research: [`docs/research/hay2010-company-activity-and-careers.md`](../../docs/research/hay2010-company-activity-and-careers.md).
- **Agreed scope:** French only; adapted corporate site with Accueil, L'Entreprise, Nos Métiers, Réalisations, Carrières, Médias, Contact + legal; drop lamalif-only contest pages; same IA and section patterns, new HAY2010 brand. The page tree, navigation and homepage order are decided in [#2](https://github.com/mustaphaelou/hay2010website/issues/2).
- **Phasing:** build and deploy to a **self-hosted Coolify stack on the developer's ARM64 VPS**; the staging URL is `https://staging.hay2010.ma` once [#15](https://github.com/mustaphaelou/hay2010website/issues/15) repairs the `hay2010.ma` delegation and puts the zone on Cloudflare. The apex/`www` public cutover remains a later launch task ([GitHub #16](https://github.com/mustaphaelou/hay2010website/issues/16)). #15 is a staging prerequisite; #16 owns the public cutover.
- **Content rule:** use verified public research; draft French copy; flag placeholders for missing photos, references and brand assets. Never publish unverified client claims or legal facts. The confirmed facts, approved wording and gated-claim list are in [`docs/company/facts-register.md`](../../docs/company/facts-register.md); copy, footer, legal pages and structured data must follow it.
- **Company identity (confirmed 2026-11-08):** SOCIETE HAY 2010 SARL, RC 87305 — Tribunal de commerce de Rabat, ICE 001459327000056, capital 15 000 000 MAD, registered 26/09/2011; the 2004/RC 517 record is a different, radiée El Kelaâ entity.
- **Existing repo:** Next.js 16.4.3 App Router, React 19, Tailwind 4, shadcn `base-maia`, Hugeicons, TypeScript. Only the template page exists.
- **Content model (resolved 2026-11-09):** **Payload 3** (MIT, self-hosted) embedded in the Next.js app, admin at `/admin`; Postgres content, S3/R2 media. Legal identity, approved claims, navigation and SEO defaults stay code-owned; the owner publishes routine content; Actualités is a gated `communiques` type. Full spec: [`docs/content/content-model.md`](../../docs/content/content-model.md).
- **Réalisations evidence (2026-11-09):** the official procurement records name HAY2010 only as a bidder — eight records, zero documented awards; Khemisset's official PV was not located and the aggregator's 14 wins / 78.66 M DH cannot be attributed. The page is credentials-led; never publish participations, losing bids or aggregator totals. See [#6](https://github.com/mustaphaelou/hay2010website/issues/6) and [`docs/content/realisations-strategy.md`](../../docs/content/realisations-strategy.md).
- **Tracker:** GitHub issues are canonical: map [#1](https://github.com/mustaphaelou/hay2010website/issues/1); tickets are sub-issues labelled `wayfinder:<type>`. This local file is a mirror; local-to-GitHub mapping is in [`github-issues.md`](github-issues.md).
- **Skills for sessions:** `grilling` + `domain-modeling` for decisions; `prototype` for visual directions; `research` for external facts.
- **Standing preferences:** decisions over deliverables; one ticket per session (except research); no build work in this map; keep this map index-only.
- **Known infrastructure risk:** hay2010.ma's DNS delegation is split between Cloudflare and HeberjaHiz; the Cloudflare authoritative servers refuse the zone, so resolvers can intermittently SERVFAIL. The decided stack (Cloudflare CDN/Access plus an R2 custom domain) requires the zone active on Cloudflare, so [#15](https://github.com/mustaphaelou/hay2010website/issues/15) is now a **staging prerequisite**, not just a launch task: recover or create the zone, copy MX/SPF/DKIM/autodiscover exactly, delegate to Cloudflare only, and verify email. The first deployment can smoke-test on a temporary protected hostname before #15. See [Research hosting, domain and deployment options](issues/06-hosting-research.md) and [Fix the hay2010.ma DNS delegation and identify account owners](issues/14-dns-delegation.md).
- **Compliance is a launch dependency:** CNDP filings are required before public forms collect real contact data or CVs; they do not block a staging build with forms disabled or test-only. Do not copy lamalif's blank receipt number or missing privacy policy. See [Research Moroccan legal and consent requirements for the site](issues/08-legal-research.md), [Decide analytics, consent and legal page content](issues/09-analytics-consent-legal.md) and [Plan the CNDP filings and launch compliance](issues/13-cndp-filings.md).
- **Legal checklist:** the legal research note's compliance checklist (consent notices, retention jobs, CV storage, security headers, rights procedure, processor contracts) is part of the build spec, not optional polish. The analytics/consent and legal-page decision is [`docs/legal/analytics-consent-legal-pages.md`](../../docs/legal/analytics-consent-legal-pages.md); the mailbox gates are in §6 there.
- **Analytics and consent (resolved 2026-11-09):** no client-side analytics at launch and no cookie banner; Cloudflare edge analytics only. Cloudflare Web Analytics after #14's F-214, cookieless, six-month retention. Any future non-essential cookie needs a lightweight banner. No newsletter.

Full spec: [`docs/content/content-model.md`](../../docs/content/content-model.md).
- [Define the réalisations and portfolio strategy](https://github.com/mustaphaelou/hay2010website/issues/6): **Credentials-led references page, not a portfolio** — the 2026-11-09 official-records check found zero documented awards; the two current ONEE agréments carry the page and `Références sur demande` stands until the owner's project inventory is documented and clients sign name/logo/photo/quote permissions. Full spec: [`docs/hosting/deployment-decision.md`](../../docs/hosting/deployment-decision.md).
- [Decide analytics, consent and legal page content](https://github.com/mustaphaelou/hay2010website/issues/10): **No analytics at launch** (Cloudflare edge analytics only; no banner); Cloudflare Web Analytics after #14's F-214, cookieless, six-month retention. ## Out of scope

- Rebuilding or migrating `lamalifgroup.ma`, or reusing its content as HAY2010 content.
- E-commerce, client portal, extranet, quoting tools.
- Multilingual versions; this effort is French-only.
- Native mobile app.
- Paid acquisition and ongoing SEO/content marketing beyond on-site SEO.
- Lamalif-only pages: Concours Madinaty, STARS IN THE PLACE, Lamalif Le Mag.
- Custom CRM/ERP integration.
- Full brand identity system beyond the web (stationery, signage, vehicle livery).
- Photo/video production by the build team. Commissioning, art direction, licensing and selection are in scope for #4; video production is out.
- Ongoing legal counsel; legal pages are drafted from research, not legal advice. The one-off review of the first communiqué is a launch gate, not an ongoing retainer.
