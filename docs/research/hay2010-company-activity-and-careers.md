# HAY2010 SARL (Rabat, Morocco) — Company Activity & Careers Research

**Last updated:** 2026-11-04
**Scope guard / disclaimer:** This report covers only **HAY2010 SARL / Société Hay 2010 (Rabat, Morocco)**. It deliberately excludes unrelated entities with similar names (Danish furniture brand HAY apS, Hays plc, Hay Group/Korn Ferry, US hay/farm-supply LLCs). Where a *same-name Moroccan entity* exists that is probably **not** this company (an SARL registered in El Kelaâ des Sraghna in 2004, later "radiée"), it is labeled explicitly as such.

---

## TL;DR

HAY2010 SARL is a long-running, small-headcount electrical-works contractor based in Rabat whose declared / self-described activity is **travaux d'éclairage public, électrification HT/MT and MT/BT, postes de transformation (transformer stations) and CFO/CFA (câbles forte/faible)** [S1][S5][S11]. It wins public contracts from Moroccan territorial authorities (verified award: *Province de Khemisset*, Marché 06/CPK/2024, 187,607 DH TTC) [S5], and a procurement aggregator attributes it **14 won tenders worth ≈78.66 M DH cumulated, mostly "Travaux" work in Rabat-Salé-Kénitra** (aggregated view, quality unverified) [S8]. Recruiting has been continuous from ≈2022 to the present week, almost entirely through LinkedIn job postings (posted by the "Hay2010 Sarl" company account) and LinkedIn/Facebook posts of the "Electrical Engineering Morocco" page, with an official mailbox **recrutement@hay2010.ma** [S14][S16][S17]. The company itself consistently tells candidates it was **"créée en 2004"** and is "agrée" (accredited), based in Rabat (Hay El Fath) [S11][S13][S15] — while the Rabat commercial registry card shows registration on **26 September 2011**, and a (radiée) same-name SARL registered **12 May 2004 in El Kelaâ des Sraghna** exists in the directories; the legal continuity between the two is not verifiable from public sources [S2][S4]. Public-facing people signals include **Rahhal Essamlali, self-described "Gérant de la ste Hay2010 Sarl"** (LinkedIn job poster) [S13][S20], a public profile listed as "Chef de projet chez Hay2010" [S21], and one listed as "Vendeur commerce de détail chez Hay2010" [S22]. The company's own website hay2010.ma exists but is still unfilled WordPress placeholder content [S18], and the GitHub `hay2010-portfolio` repo mentioned in earlier briefs returns 404 at retrieval time [S19].

### 1.4 Discrepancies — explicit flag list

1. **Founding year: "créée en 2004" (company self-description in hiring posts) vs. Rabat registration 26 September 2011.**
   The 2004 date matches an SARL « SOCIÉTÉ HAY 2010 » **registered 12 May 2004 at the Tribunal of El Kelaâ des Sraghna** (capital 500,000 MAD), which is now **radiée** [S4] and not verifiably the same legal person as the Rabat entity. The same El Kelaâ entity's declared activity ("marchand import/export, entrepreneur de travaux divers ou constructions, marchand de machine de bureau en détail, négociant, entrepreneur d'installations électriques") is stylistically the ancestor of the Rabat SARL's object [S3][S4]. Possible explanations (all unverified): relocation/re-registration of the same business, a new SARL by the same shareholders keeping the name, or sloppy self-description in job ads. **Needs an RC certificate to settle.**
2. **Capital: 15,000,000 MAD (live Charika + live Welipro main card) vs. 6,000,000 MAD (live Welipro "HAY 2010" card RC 87305) vs. 500,000 MAD (2004 El Kelaâ radiée card) vs. 4,700,000 MAD (figure noted in the research brief, attributed to a Welipro snapshot — not confirmed by any page live on 2026-11-04).**
   The most plausible reading (aggregator snapshots at different dates) is a capital that changed over time (capital-increase events 2015, 2016, 2018, 2026 are logged [S1]); the 11 Mar 2026 event includes an *augmentation du capital* consistent with a step to the current 15 MDH figure [S1]. The 4.7M brief value is treated here as a stale/older snapshot and is NOT reported as current fact.
3. **RC number: 517 (Rabat) on the "SOCIÉTÉ HAY 2010" card vs. 87305 (Rabat) on the second "HAY 2010" card.**
   Both cards point to Imm. 26 Sept 2011 / the same Ribat Al Fath address zone and the same activity wording; BT Africa cross-params RC 87305 with ICE 001459327000056 (the ICE shown on the main card) [S2][S3][S9]. Notably, RC 517 at *Rabat* is low for a 2011 registration (comparably-sized Rabat companies registered in 2011 have 5-digit RCs, e.g. "HAY 2010" RC 87305 [S3]), so one of the two Welipro cards may be a mis-associated record. **Cannot be resolved from aggregator pages — verify with an official RC extract (Tribunal de commerce de Rabat).**
4. **Address fragmentation:** three Rabat-area addresses coexist (Hay El Manzeh/Hay Er-Riad [S1]; Ribat Al Fath CYM/Yacoub El Mansour [S2][S3]; Hay El Fath [S10][S13][S14]); the 09 May 2018 and **11 Mar 2026 "transfert du siège social" events** in Charika confirm the registered seat has moved at least twice [S1].
5. **The 03/06/2025 "Commune urbaine de TAMALLALT" award listed under the name "HAY2010 SARL"** [S7] is non-electrical (hospitality/restoration services) and geographically distant from Rabat but adjacent to El Kelaâ des Sraghna — where the radiée 2004 SARL was registered [S4]. Attribution identity is uncertain; treat **as probably a different "Hay 2010"** unless confirmed (see §3).

## 2. Business activity

### 2.1 What they do (multiple corroborating sources)

- **Registry activity line (Charika, primary descriptive field):** *"travaux d'éclairage public, electrification ht/mt, mt/bt et postes de transformations, cfo/cfa"* — i.e. **public lighting works, high/medium & medium/low-voltage electrification, transformer stations (postes de transformation), strong/weak-current cabling (CFO/CFA)** [S1].
- **Registry activity line (Welipro main card):** *"negociant, md effectuant importation exportation, entrepreneur de travaux divers et entrepreneur electriques"* — the classic mixed Moroccan object combining trading activities (import/export) with **travaux divers (TD) and electrical contracting** [S2]; mirrored by BT Africa's index of the same card [S9]. The second Welipro card adds *"marchand de machine de bureau en détail"* (an office-machines retail clause) and *"entrepreneur d'installations électriques"* [S3].
- **Self-description to job candidates:** *"HAY2010 SARL est une société créée en 2004, opérant dans le domaine d'Électricité, énergetique et éclairage (Éclairage public, Lignes MT, Postes de transformation, énergetique, solaire, …) à Rabat"* (2026 wording, note **solar/énergie added**) [S11]; earlier wording: *"opérant dans le domaine d'Électricité HTA-BT (Éclairage public, Lignes MT, Postes de transformation, …)"* (≈2023) [S12] and *"HAY2010, une entreprise fondée en 2004 et spécialisée dans le domaine de l'électrification (éclairage public, éclairage de bâtiments, CFO/CFA, lignes MT, postes de transformation, etc.) à Rabat"* (≈2024) [S15]. A 2025–2026 posting for a Purchases & Logistics manager calls it an *"entreprise agrée spécialisée dans l'électrification et l'éclairage public"* [S13][S14] (the "agrée" = officially accredited claim is **self-asserted**; no accreditation document was found publicly).
- Delivery posture evidenced by role requirements: in-house engineering of *maîtrise d'œuvre* documentation, budgeting/chiffrage, **public-lighting photometric studies with DIALux**, AutoCAD, site supervision and compliance control [S11][S12] — i.e. a contracting company with its own design/cost-control functions, not a pure-works subcontractor.

### 2.2 Clients & markets

- **Public buyers (verified):** *Province de KHEMISSET* — supply & laying of public-lighting cable (Marché 06/CPK/2024) [S5]. Khemisset is in the Rabat-Salé-Kénitra region, consistent with the company's registry base.
- **Geography (aggregated):** procurement aggregator Pyramide Marchés shows dominant sector **"Travaux"**, main region **Rabat-Salé-Kénitra** [S8]. A ≈2024 site-foreman hire was advertised for **Casablanca (Casablanca-Settat)** [S15], indicating at least occasional work in the Casablanca region; job postings advertise "mobilité nationale" for engineers [S11][S12].
- **Tender environment:** the two individually documented awards attracted **16** and **14** bidders respectively [S5][S7] — typical of small municipal BDC/Bon de commande works in Morocco.
- **ONEE (Office National de l'Électricité et de l'Eau potable) / public-utility contractor lists:** no mention of HAY2010 was found in publicly indexed sources checked on 2026-11-04 (no ONEE contractor roster match surfaced) — see Open Questions.

## 6. Open questions / gaps

2. **Legal continuity 2004 → 2011.** Whether the 2004 El Kelaâ SARL (radiée) and the 2011 Rabat SARL are legally the same business (transfer of seat / continuation vs. a new company) [S2][S4].
3. **Gérant / associates per registry.** Charika paywall blocks the "Représentants légaux" data; LinkedIn's self-declaration [S13] is the only public name. Verify with a Charika "Rapport Express/Gold" or equivalent before publishing a gérant name as registry fact.
4. **Award list from the official portal.** The 14-win / 78.66 M DH figures [S8] come from an aggregator; and the Tamallalt 2025 award [S7] is likely a namesake. Pulling the official result notices (attributions) directly from marchespublics.gov.ma (guest search or open-data endpoint) would let us enumerate actual projects (province/commune, works type) and confirm entity matching.
5. **ONEE / utility contractor rosters.** Nothing surfaced in indexed sources linking HAY2010 to ONEE (ONEE-BT) lists; absence is weaker than presence because the aggregated Web coverage of such PDF/lists is sparse. Directly search onee.ma lists / jurisdictional official bulletins (Bulletin Officiel / marchespublics.gov.ma) to close this.
6. **Portfolio website content.** hay2010.ma is confirmed as the company's own domain (used for recrutement@hay2010.ma [S14]) but the site is unburned WordPress placeholder content (generic construction-services template with "Lorem ipsum" everywhere; first post "Hello world!" on 16 Feb 2026; contact page shows US-template fictional address "12345 Porto Blvd., Los Angeles") [S18]. 7. **Headcount / CNSS.** No public headcount (CNSS-affilié counts are not surfaced by free sources); LinkedIn "colleagues" counts (3 and 17) are soft proxies [S20][S22].
8. **Financials.** Bilans 2021–2023 available via Charika but behind payment [S1]; not readable without a purchase — revenue/employee figures remain unverified.
