# CDC deviation register

Every divergence between the cahier des charges and what ships is recorded here and needs the structeur's written sign-off before it is built. This register covers **divergences from the CDC only**: reversals of earlier repository decisions that the CDC does not speak to (for example dropping the gated Médiathèque) are recorded in the ticket and ADRs, not here.

Authority: `docs/adr/0001-cahier-des-charges-governs-structure.md`. Evidence authority: `docs/company/facts-register.md`.

Status: accepted internally, **pending structeur sign-off** on every row.

| ID | CDC clause | Deviation as implemented | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| DEV-001 | §2.4 chiffres clés, §2.5 réalisations sélectionnées, §5.1–5.3 catalogue and project sheets | Réalisations launches credentials-led: `Références sur demande`, zero project cards, no filters, no per-project pages, no project photo galleries; the homepage carries no selected-project cards and no stats strip until verifiable figures exist | The 2026-11-09 official-records check names HAY2010 only as a bidder — eight records, zero documented awards; client names, logos, photos and testimonials are gated on written permission (`docs/content/realisations-strategy.md`, facts register §6) |
